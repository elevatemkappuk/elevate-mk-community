# Elevate MK Community: business overview

## Executive overview

Elevate MK Community is the member-facing digital space for Elevate MK. It
provides the foundation for members to join Elevate MK, establish secure
access to their Community account, view their membership profile and keep
selected personal and professional information up to date.

Members can maintain their name, location, professional information, bio,
skills and interests. The experience is connected to the existing Elevate MK
membership and CRM information, so Community is designed to strengthen one
shared member record rather than create a competing one.

This is the current foundation for a broader member Community experience.
Members can now discover eligible profiles through Connect, view member
profiles, manage established connections and process incoming or sent
connection requests. Home request attention integration remains future C4B
scope.

## The member journey today

```text
Discover Elevate MK
        → Join
        → Membership established
        → Account setup email
        → Set password
        → Enter Community
        → View profile
        → Review or update details
        → Continue using Community
```

1. **Discover Elevate MK** — A prospective member chooses to join the
   Community.
2. **Join** — They provide the information needed to create or safely match
   their Elevate MK membership record.
3. **Membership established** — The submission is accepted and connected to
   the organisation's membership information.
4. **Account setup email** — The member receives an invitation to create
   online access.
5. **Set password** — They use the secure setup link to choose a password.
6. **Enter Community** — The member can sign in and access their member
   experience.
7. **View profile** — My Profile brings together relevant membership,
   personal and professional information.
8. **Review or update details** — The member can correct supported
   information and confirm that existing details are up to date.
9. **Continue using Community** — The member can return to their profile and
   account whenever needed.

## Joining Elevate MK

The current Join experience captures:

- first name and last name;
- gender and age range;
- email address;
- optional mobile number;
- location;
- industry;
- current role or job title;
- optional LinkedIn profile; and
- optional email marketing consent.

This native Join experience gives Elevate MK a direct Community membership
journey and reduces reliance on disconnected intake processes. It does not
mean that the public website has already been changed from any earlier form
or process; that is a separate operational decision.

Join is designed to work with information Elevate MK may already hold about
the person. It does not reveal to the person whether a matching internal
record exists.

## Existing members and existing records

Elevate MK may already know a person because they joined through an earlier
process, appeared in historical membership information or already exist in
the CRM.

Where it is safe to do so, Community reuses the existing person and member
record instead of creating a duplicate identity. If the information supplied
does not provide a safe answer, or appears contradictory, the submission is
handled conservatively and may require review. This protects the accuracy of
the member record without exposing internal matching details.

## Membership

Community is connected to the same underlying Elevate MK membership
information used by the organisation. It does not create a separate
"Community membership" that competes with the CRM membership record.

- A new accepted member can establish an active membership.
- An existing active member keeps their existing membership relationship.
- A former member is not silently reactivated through the normal Join
  journey.

Membership and online account access are related but different. A person can
have an eligible membership relationship before completing online account
setup.

## Account setup

After a successful Join, the member is invited to set up their Community
account:

```text
Join completed
    → account setup email
    → secure setup link
    → member chooses a password
    → Community access becomes available
```

The member does not need to choose a password during the initial Join form.
An existing membership can therefore exist without an online Community
account until the member completes setup.

## Sign in and account recovery

The current member access experience supports:

- signing in;
- signing out;
- remaining signed in appropriately between visits;
- requesting a forgotten-password link; and
- securely resetting a password.

Password recovery deliberately gives the same safe outcome whether or not a
particular email address belongs to an eligible account. It does not reveal
account registration information.

## My Profile

My Profile is the member's consolidated, owner-facing view of information
relevant to Elevate MK membership and Community participation. It currently
includes:

- name and location;
- professional information;
- Community bio;
- skills and interests;
- membership context;
- optional private profile photo; and
- profile completion progress.

It is intended to become the member's place for maintaining their Elevate MK
information over time.

## What members can update

Members can currently update:

**Personal information**

- first name;
- last name; and
- location.

**Professional information**

- job title;
- company;
- industry;
- career stage; and
- LinkedIn profile.

**Community information**

- bio;
- skills; and
- interests.

Members can also upload, change, or remove a private profile photo. JPEG, PNG,
and WebP files up to 5 MiB are accepted for client-side feedback; backend
validation remains authoritative. There is no cropper in V1, initials are used
as a fallback, and profile photos do not affect Profile Progress.

Skills and Interests use Elevate MK's managed choices rather than unrestricted
free-text entries. This keeps information consistent for the current Connect
discovery and networking experience.

## Information members cannot currently change

Not every part of the member record is self-service. Through My Profile,
members cannot currently change:

- email address;
- mobile number;
- gender;
- age range;
- membership status; or
- marketing preferences.

Internal organisational information such as staff notes, internal tags,
administrative records and staff roles remains outside the member profile.

## Reviewing existing information

Some members already existed in Elevate MK records before creating their
Community account. After signing in, these members may see a **Check your
details** prompt asking them to review the information already associated
with them.

They can either:

- review and update their profile; or
- confirm that the information **Looks good**.

This provides a practical way to improve existing member data over time. It
is not a field-by-field conflict-resolution process. The member sees their
current profile after authentication and decides whether it needs updating.

## Profile completion

Profile Progress helps members understand whether important areas of their
profile are complete. The current areas are:

- name;
- professional details;
- bio;
- skills; and
- interests.

A more complete profile creates a stronger foundation for future Community
features. Completion does not currently determine membership eligibility or
access.

## One member record across Elevate MK

Community was deliberately designed not to create a separate copy of each
member. Where information belongs to the core Elevate MK member record,
Community works with that same information.

When a member updates supported information such as their name through
Community, Elevate MK staff can see the updated canonical information in the
CRM as well. This provides:

- fewer duplicate records;
- less conflicting member information;
- an opportunity for members to keep their own information current;
- one shared foundation for staff and members; and
- a stronger base for future Elevate MK services.

## Community and CRM

Community and CRM serve different audiences:

| Experience | Audience | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community | Members | Join, access the account and maintain supported personal and professional information. |
| CRM | Elevate MK staff | Operate the organisation's member and relationship records. |

Community members do not receive access to the Staff CRM. Staff-only
information remains staff-only. The two experiences share underlying member
information where appropriate, while keeping their audiences and purposes
separate.

## Communication relationship

Selected member information can continue to flow through the existing
Elevate MK communication process. For example, when a member updates their
name through Community, the corresponding contact information can be
synchronized through the established communication integration.

Marketing consent remains separate from ordinary profile editing. Updating
a profile does not change a member's marketing choice.

## Privacy principles

The current product is designed around these principles:

- members access only their own Community profile;
- staff-only CRM information is not shown to Community members;
- joining does not reveal whether a particular person already exists in
  Elevate MK's internal records;
- uncertain identity matches are handled conservatively;
- account recovery does not reveal whether an email address is registered;
  and
- Community access depends on an eligible Elevate MK membership
  relationship.

## What the current release gives Elevate MK

**A native digital Join journey** — A direct Community intake experience
reduces dependence on disconnected membership processes.

**A member account** — Members can establish secure digital access to their
Community experience.

**Member self-service** — Members can maintain selected personal,
professional and Community information themselves.

**Improved data quality** — Existing members can review and update
information already held by Elevate MK.

**One member identity** — Community and CRM work from the same core member
record rather than maintaining separate versions of the member.

**Structured professional, skills and interests information** — Managed
information creates a useful foundation for future member discovery and
networking.

**Account recovery** — Members have a sustainable way to regain access
without routine staff intervention.

These are current product capabilities, not measured claims about savings,
engagement or conversion.

## What is not built yet

The following are not part of the current Community product:

- profile photo;
- QR profile sharing;
- messaging;
- an events or community event participation experience;
- member-to-member contact features;
- self-service email changes;
- self-service mobile changes; and
- a full Account Settings area.

## Where the product is heading

The current release establishes the foundation for a richer Community
product. Potential future areas include QR profile sharing, messaging,
events and Home request attention integration. These are future possibilities
or planned areas, not current capabilities.
